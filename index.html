The Problem

Our choice has been beauty or pain, until now.

The last structural innovation in the heel was the stiletto, 70 years ago.

More than 61% of women who wear heels carry a second pair to get through the day. The category has seen decades of design iteration — new silhouettes, new materials, new brands — but the fundamental tension has never been addressed.

Every woman who has changed shoes before a long walk, packed three pairs for one day's events, or chosen flats because she couldn't risk the pain — she has been waiting for this.

Our Vision

The desire for transformation is by design.

THYS is not a shoe company. It is the object a woman reaches for when she refuses to choose between who she is and where she's going.

The first luxury heel engineered for women who control their environment — not the other way around. Technology and craft, inseparable by design.
