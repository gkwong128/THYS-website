The Problem

Our choice has been beauty or pain, until now.

The last structural innovation in the heel was the stiletto, 70 years ago.

And it hasn't seen a meaningful product innovation at the luxury tier since. Decades of design iteration, but our fundamental tension remains unsolved. Enter THYS.

Today, the global footwear market is worth $120 billion. Every woman who has changed shoes before a long walk, packed three pairs for the day's events, or chosen flats because she couldn't risk the pain -- she has been waiting for an answer.

Our Vision

The desire for transformation is by design.

Heels that adjust to you. Wear one pair. Do everything.

THYS is building the first shoe that adapts to its wearer, not the other way around. We believe technology and luxury are not in opposition. They are the future of fashion.

The Product

Built around you. Down to the millimetre.

True to Form

Dimensions researched and developed to match the natural shape of real feet — so every pair fits the way a shoe always should.

Precision Height

Not just high or low. Dial in the exact height you want, when you want it — and change your mind whenever you do.

Luxury by Design

Every material, silhouette, and finish is crafted to stand alongside the world's finest footwear. Beautiful from every angle.

Silent Adjustment

The mechanism is whisper-quiet and completely discreet. The only person who knows it just happened is you.
