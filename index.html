The Problem

The last structural innovation in the heel was the stiletto, 70 years ago.
Our choice has been beauty or pain, until now.

The global footwear market is worth $120 billion — and it hasn't seen a meaningful product innovation at the luxury tier since the invention of the stiletto in the 1950s. Decades of design iteration, but the fundamental tension remains unsolved.

Every woman who has changed shoes before a long walk, packed three pairs for a five-day trip, or chosen flats because she couldn't risk the pain — she has been waiting for an answer. Until now.

Our Vision

The desire for transformation is by design.

Heels that adjust to you. Wear one pair. Do everything.

THYS is building the first shoe that adapts to its wearer — not the other way around. We believe technology and luxury are not in opposition. They are the future of fashion.
