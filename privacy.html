Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

THYS ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Waitlist sign-ups: your name and email address when you join our early access waitlist.
  • Investor inquiries: your name, email address, and any information you choose to include when contacting us at info@thys.co.
  • General correspondence: any information you send us directly by email or through our contact page.

We do not collect payment information, and we do not use cookies or tracking technologies on this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Notify you about the THYS product launch and early access availability.
  • Respond to your investor inquiries and general questions.
  • Send occasional updates about THYS — you may opt out at any time by replying to any email with "unsubscribe."

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted on Netlify. When you submit a form, your data is processed through Netlify's infrastructure. Netlify may log basic request data (such as IP addresses) in accordance with their own Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent or opt out of communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@thys.co.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at info@thys.co.
